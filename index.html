Home Dashboard

Home Dashboard is a private, single-user app. It runs on its owner's home server and shows a daily summary: orders to ship, emails waiting for a reply, packages arriving, the family calendar, the weather and the server's health.

It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

It uses Google sign-in with read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It never sends, changes or deletes mail or calendar events.
