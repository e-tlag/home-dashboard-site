Privacy policy

Home Dashboard · Last updated November 20, 2026

Who uses this app

Home Dashboard is a private app used only by its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it reads

With the owner's permission, the app requests two read-only Google scopes:

  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): to read notification emails about orders, packages and backups, and to count email threads that need a reply.
  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): to show today's events from the owner's calendars.

The app never sends, labels, archives, moves or deletes email, and it never creates or changes calendar events.

What it stores

The app stores only the few fields it needs to display: for example an item title, a deadline, a tracking number, a sender's name, an email subject and event times. It does not store full email bodies or attachments. The data is kept in a local database on the owner's home server, which is reachable only over the owner's private network.

Sharing

Data obtained through Google APIs is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, and it is not used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google Account → Third-party apps & services. Deleting the app's database on the home server removes all stored data.

Contact

Questions about this app can go to the support email shown on its Google sign-in screen.
